About this book

How US and international companies build a business credit history from absolute zero, and why it matters even if you never plan to borrow. The guide covers the Dun & Bradstreet system, the Paydex score, net-30 trade credit, business credit cards without a personal guarantee, and a strategy for reaching a strong business credit rating in 6–12 months.
