About this book

A practical guide to selling to the US federal government as a small business. It covers registration in the System for Award Management (SAM), UEI and CAGE codes, NAICS classification, set-aside programs such as 8(a), SDVOSB, WOSB, and HUBZone, contract search, capability statements, SBIR and STTR funding, proposal writing, and building relationships with contracting officers.

Translated from the Russian. Prepared with the help of AI tools.
