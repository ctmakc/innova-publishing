About this book

A practical guide to organic growth for SaaS and service businesses, updated in November 2026. It explains how Google AI Overviews and AI Mode work, how search works in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Copilot, and what to do so your brand is named in AI answers (GEO). It also covers technical SEO, content, links and mentions, local search, analytics, and turning organic traffic into paying customers. Facts are checked against documentation from Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, and Microsoft.
